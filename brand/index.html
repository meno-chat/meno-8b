Meno is an open-weight tutoring model in development. It's being built to give hints, not answers.

status: in development, nothing released yet
01 / mascot

one face, three moods

A rounded face with two eyes. Only the expression changes: thinking, got it, and not quite. There are no accessories.

thinkingDefault. Pupils up and right, soft smile.
got itClosed happy eyes, wider smile.
not quitePupils down and left, small “o” mouth.
02 / logo

the mark and the wordmark

The face on mint is the primary mark. The wordmark is always lowercase: meno. Keep clear space equal to one eye width on every side.

05 / voice

ask first, then tell

Short sentences. Questions before explanations. Point at the step that went wrong, never at the student.

2x + 3 = 11. just tell me x
not yet. what could you do to both sides first?
subtract 3. so 2x = 8, and x = 8?
2x = 8 is right. now, 2 times what gives 8?
oh. x = 4
there it is.

Hand-written example for illustration. It is not model output.

Proposed side line: ask meno. it'll ask you back.

06 / components

flat, round, pressable

Buttons are flat color with a thick darker bottom edge. Corners are 16px on buttons and 24px on cards. No gradients and no blur.

One dominant colorEvery screen is mostly mint or mostly ink.
Ink on mintNever white text on mint.
Lowercase wordmark“meno” is always lowercase. No all caps in headlines.
One faceThe mascot is never rotated, stretched, or recolored outside the palette.
